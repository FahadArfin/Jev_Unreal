# Community code of conduct

We want people of different backgrounds and experience levels to be able to build,
learn, and disagree constructively here.

Be respectful. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Give useful
feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and respect other people's time and boundaries.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share someone else's private information,
or post sexual content or deliberate spam in project spaces.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation.
Responses should be proportional to the behavior, from a request to change conduct
to a temporary or permanent ban for serious or repeated misconduct.

To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method on
their GitHub profile. If no private contact is available, request one in an issue
without identifying affected people or sharing sensitive details. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical. Do not retaliate against someone for reporting
a concern in good faith.
